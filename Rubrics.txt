Judging Rubric
A panel of judges scores each team across five weighted categories. Every category is scored on a 1–5 scale, and the weighted totals determine the winners.

Categories
1. Technical Execution and Functionality
30%
Does the product actually work? Judges look at how well the build runs end-to-end, the quality of the implementation, and how much of the proposed solution was delivered during the weekend.

2. Problem Clarity and Solution Fit
20%
Is the problem clearly defined and worth solving? Judges evaluate how well the team understands the target user and how directly the solution addresses the stated problem.

3. Market Viability and Financial Logic
20%
Could this become a real business? Judges consider the size of the opportunity, who would pay, and whether the basic financial model (pricing, costs, revenue) makes sense.

4. Impact and Growth Potential
20%
How big could this get? Judges weigh the potential impact on users or industry, and how realistically the team could grow the product beyond the hackathon.

5. Presentation and Professionalism
10%
Can the team tell the story well? Judges score the clarity of the demo, the quality of the pitch, and how confidently the team communicates their vision.

Scoring Scale
Each category is scored on a scale of 1–5:

5
Exceptional
Outstanding work that clearly stands out
4
Strong
Above average with clear strengths
3
Solid
Meets expectations across the board
2
Limited
Some effort but notable gaps
1
Minimal
Little evidence in this area
How judging works: Each judge scores every team 1–5 in each category. Scores are weighted by category percentage and combined across the panel. Winners are announced Sunday evening at closing.
